Power distribution

Two independent battery paths: a regulated 3V3 logic rail, and a raw, unregulated feed reserved for the cellular modem's transmit-current spikes.

Design noteThe modem's battery feed (BATT_LTE_RAW) bypasses the boost/LDO chain entirely — sensible, since the A7670C can pulse 1–2 A during transmit and forcing that through a small LDO would brown out the logic rail.
